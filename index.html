About

I am an incoming master’s student at Northwest University, China. My research interests lie in computer vision, particularly 3D reconstruction, Gaussian splatting, and physical scene understanding. I have also worked on reviewing generative world models for interactive games.

Research Interests

3D ReconstructionGaussian SplattingPhysical Scene UnderstandingGenerative World Models

Dynamic 3D reconstruction

My research experience includes studying physical consistency in dynamic Gaussian splatting, implementing optical-flow alignment and motion constraints, and constructing synthetic datasets with physics-based rendering tools.

World models for interactive games

I have reviewed autoregressive world models, focusing on model architectures, their challenges, and approaches to interactive scene generation.

Technical Background

Python, PyTorch, NumPy, and Matplotlib; deep learning and Transformer architectures. Experience with research implementation, experimental evaluation, and scientific writing.
